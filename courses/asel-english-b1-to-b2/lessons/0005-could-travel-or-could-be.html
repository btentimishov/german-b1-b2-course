Как быстро выбрать модель?

Если после could нужно действие, ставь обычный глагол: could travel, could sleep. Если нужно описание, добавь be: could be flexible, could be comfortable.

Where does far go? Travel far means travel a long distance. For a destination, say travel to Berlin or drive to Berlin. far = далеко, а не ехать.

Grammar check: the British Council shows modal + be in examples such as “It will be dark,” and Cambridge explains that adjectives follow be while adverbs commonly describe actions. British Council: The verb be · Cambridge: Adverbs—forms.
