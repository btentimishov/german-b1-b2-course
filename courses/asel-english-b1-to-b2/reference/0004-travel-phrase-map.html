Four travel phrases

First choose the meaning: how you travel, which transport you choose, or where you are during the journey.

→

Method · способ

by train · by car

We travelled by train.

✓

Let’s take the train.

Давай поедем на поезде.
▭

●

Position · место

in the car

The three-question decision

  1. 1
    How did you travel?by + transport, no article: by train
  2. 2
    Which transport did you choose?take + the/a + transport: take the train
  3. 3
    Where were you?on public transport; in a car or taxi
