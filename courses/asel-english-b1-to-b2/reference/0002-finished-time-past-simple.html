Asel’s quick reference · Story grammar

Finished time → past simple

When the time is over and named, use the past simple for the main events. Let the time marker place the listener first; then move through the events in order.

→
Last Saturday, I planned dinner.finished time + subject + past verb + detail

Markers that close the time

yesterdaylast nightlast weekendon Mondaytwo days agoin 2024

Different signal: since Monday connects Monday to now, so it normally needs the present perfect: “I have felt tired since Monday.” We will practise that contrast in a later lesson.

Three useful forms

+

Something happened

I prepared snacks. We had guests.

−

Something did not happen

I didn’t prepare dinner. After didn’t, use the base verb.

?

Ask about a finished event

Did you call your friend? After did, use the base verb.

The four-line story recipe

  1. 1
    Place the listenerLast Sunday, …
  2. 2
    Give the planI planned / wanted / decided to …
  3. 3
    Add the problemBut …
  4. 4
