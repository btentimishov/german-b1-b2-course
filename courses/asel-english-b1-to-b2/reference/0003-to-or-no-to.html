To or no to?

Look at the first verb. A plan, decision, wish, or need normally takes to + base verb. A core modal goes directly to the base verb.

Bridge: to

plan · decide · want · need · hope

verb + to + base verb

We planned to play.
She decided to leave.

планировали поиграть · решила уйти

No bridge

can · could · should · must

modal + base verb

We could wait.
You should rest.

могли подождать · следует отдохнуть

The two-line memory card

to
planned to go · decided to stayПосле plan / decide / want / need ставим to.
Ø
could go · should stayПосле can / could / should / must слово to не ставим.

Quick repairs

Try againNatural English
We planned go.We planned to go.
We decided play.We decided to play.
The baby could to stay.The baby could stay.
You should to rest.You should rest.

A four-part story frame

  1. 1
    Last weekend, we planned to…Когда? Какой был план?
  2. 2
    But … could not…Какая появилась проблема?
  3. 3
    So we decided to…Какое новое решение вы приняли?
  4. 4
    In the end,…Чем всё закончилось?

Useful chunk: instead of + -ing = вместо того чтобы…
We went to the playground instead of playing tennis.

Checked against British Council: Verbs followed by the infinitive and Cambridge Grammar: Infinitives with and without to.
