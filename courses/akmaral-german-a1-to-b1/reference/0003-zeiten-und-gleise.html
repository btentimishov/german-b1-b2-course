Three anchors: time · platform · change

You do not need every word in an announcement. Listen for a precise time, a platform after Gleis, and a change signal such as Verspätung, statt, sondern, or entfällt.

03 / LISTENING ROUTINE

Two plays, two jobs.

  1. First play: main route.Where is the train going?
  2. Second play: anchors.Catch time, platform, and one change.
  3. Repeat the detail.Um 14:20 Uhr, von Gleis neun.
  4. Act on it.Write or say the updated plan.
