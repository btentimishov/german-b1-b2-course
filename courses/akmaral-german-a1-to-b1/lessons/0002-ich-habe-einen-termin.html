02

4 MIN · NOTICE

The noun has a passport.

NAME FORM

der Termin

A masculine noun stored with its article.
→
AFTER HABEN

Ich habe einen Termin.

The action points at the appointment.

der / ein becomes den / einen when the masculine noun is the direct target. Feminine eine and neuter ein do not change here.

First answer = evidence. Choose once. If it is incorrect, the lesson reveals the correction, saves your original choice, and lets you continue.

2. Was brauchst du für den Kurs?

03

3 MIN · BUILD MEMORY

Stamp six useful nouns.

Say the front aloud. Tap the card. Then say the new phrase before moving on.

Memory trick: learn the noun with der. That tiny “passport” tells you when einen is needed.

04

5 MIN · CONTROL ROOM

Choose the correct article.

Use the noun’s gender and the sentence job. Not every object needs einen.

Complete every line, then check once. Corrections do not block your progress.

05

4 MIN · LISTEN & RECALL

Hear it. Hide it. Retrieve it.

English hint: Play each line twice. First listen for the time or purpose. Second listen for einen + noun.

Now complete each phrase from memory. No model above contains these exact three pairs together.
