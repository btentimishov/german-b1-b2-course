The road from A2 to B2 is not one giant leap.

It is a series of increasingly flexible things you can do: first handle familiar moments, then connect stories and opinions, then communicate with range and nuance. Grammar and vocabulary are tools for those actions.

A2

Make everyday English dependable

Understand short, clear language about familiar needs. Describe routines, recent events, people, places, quantities, and simple plans. Ask for clarification when needed.

B1

Connect experiences, reasons, and plans

Deal with most familiar situations independently. Tell a connected story, explain an opinion briefly, follow the main points of clear speech, and write straightforward connected text.

B2

Communicate spontaneously and with nuance

Understand the main ideas of complex texts, interact with enough fluency for sustained conversation, and give clear, detailed viewpoints with advantages, disadvantages, contrast, and qualification.

The weekly rhythm
Learn one useful pattern in context. Retrieve it without help. Use it in a true sentence. Return after 1, 3, 7, 14, and 30 days. Mix it with older material. Once a week, use it with a real person.
