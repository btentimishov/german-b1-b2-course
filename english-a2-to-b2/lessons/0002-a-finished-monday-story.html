1 · Keep the meaning

Your story already had a strong shape.

You gave us a plan, a problem, and a result. We will keep that meaning and make the finished events easier to follow.

On Monday, we had guests. I wanted to make snacks and bake a cake, but it was not easy with a baby. My husband planned to go to the gym, so I took care of the baby and prepared the food. It was complicated, but I did it.
♥This is your voice, polished—not replaced. Your original vocabulary and story order did the important communicative work.

2 · Notice one rule

A closed time opens the past simple.

When the time is finished and named, tell the main events with the past simple.
