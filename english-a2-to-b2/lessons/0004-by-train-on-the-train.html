1 · Your evidence · about 2 minutes

Your grammar bridge held.

You used planned to… and decided to take… with the bridge to. You also used could be and could go without to. The journey still followed a clear plan → problem → decision → benefit.

Last weekend, we planned to travel to Switzerland, but the journey by car with a baby was too long. So we decided to take the train instead of going by car. On the train, things could be easier: the baby could walk around and try to talk to other people.
✓Your Lesson 3 pattern is demonstrated. Today we are polishing the travel phrases around those correct verb patterns.
Что уже получилось хорошо?

planned to… и decided to take… — правильно: после этих глаголов есть to. could be и could go — тоже правильно: после could нет to. Порядок истории понятный и логичный.

4
by train · take the train · on the train · in the carThe article and preposition change because the meaning changes.
Короткое объяснение по-русски

by train / by car отвечает на вопрос «как? каким способом?» — без артикля. take the train — выбрать или использовать поезд. Когда мы уже внутри транспорта: on the train, но in the car.

Small country note: say to Switzerland, not to the Switzerland. С большинством названий стран артикль the не нужен.

Grammar check: Cambridge distinguishes by train as the method from on the train as the place during travel. Read Cambridge Grammar: By.
