2 · See the contrast · about 3 minutes

Some verbs need a bridge. Modals do not.

When one verb expresses a plan, choice, wish, or need, connect it to the next action with to. After a modal verb, use the base verb directly.

Use the bridge

plan / decide / want / need

verb + to + base verb

We planned to play.
We decided to leave.

No bridge

can / could / should / must

modal + base verb

The baby could stay.
We should leave.

to
planned to go · could staySay both aloud. Let your ear notice where the bridge appears.
Почему иногда нужно to?

После plan, decide, want, need перед следующим глаголом ставим to: decided to go. После can, could, should, must слово to не ставим: could stay. Форму could to stay не используем.

Grammar check: British Council lists plan and decide with the to-infinitive; Cambridge Grammar explains that modal verbs take the infinitive without to.
